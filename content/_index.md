Just Objects B.V. is the trading name for the professional 
activities of <a href="https://mapstodon.space/@justb4" target="_blank">Just van den Broecke</a>.

Just Objects provides services for
<a title="GIS, Geospatial IT, or Geo-ICT" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographic_information_system" target="_blank">Geospatial IT</a>, also known as GIS or Geo-ICT, with a focus on <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software" target="_blank">Free and Open Source</a> (FOSS), Open Standards and Open Data.

The range of services include: consultancy, training, development, hosting and maintenance.
Just Objects provides three commercial cloud services built with Open Source and Open Data:

  * [map5.nl](/map5) - online maps, including the award-winning [map5topo](https://map5topo.nl) map
  * [geotoko.nl](/geotoko) - ready-to-use Dutch Open Geospatial datasets
  * [geoqos.com](/geoqos) - monitoring and QoS checking for your (OGC-based) geospatial web services


Just Objects was a member of the <a title="OpenGeoGroep" href="https://opengeogroep.nl" target="_blank">OpenGeoGroep Cooperation</a>.

Just is an <a href="https://osgeo.org" target="_blank">OSGeo</a> <a href="https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Just_van_den_Broecke" target="_blank">Charter member</a> 
and founder/former chair
of the <a title="OSGeo.nl" href="https://osgeo.nl" target="_blank">OSGeo.nl Foundation</a>, the Dutch Local Chapter of [OSGeo.org][1].

He is a regular mapper for [OpenStreetMap - OSM](https://www.openstreetmap.org/user/justb) and active in the Dutch and Spanish OSM-communities.
