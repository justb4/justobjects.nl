[OSGeo.nl](https://osgeo.nl) has been nominated for the most prestigious geo-award within The Netherlands: 
the Geo Prestige Award 2026. This is a callout to our community: vote for us!

{{< a-img data-href="https://geomares.typeform.com/to/YUyu9jo8" data-src="/uploads/2026/vote-osgeonl-gin.jpg" data-caption="vote link" >}}

TL;DR: Vote directly via [https://geomares.typeform.com/to/YUyu9jo8](https://geomares.typeform.com/to/YUyu9jo8) or click image above, or use QR. 
Sorry, form is in Dutch.

The professional association [Geo-Informatie Nederland](https://geoinformatienederland.nl/) (GIN) presents annual industry awards known as the Geo Prestige Awards. 
There are three categories: Organization, Professional and Startup. 
OSGeo.NL has been nominated in the Organization category.

We’ve been nominated... but naturally, we want to win! 
And since we are the only community-based organization (the other nominees are companies), we’re asking everyone to vote for us!
We have been active, an assocation since 2011, in many respects, most natably our anual [FOSS4G.nl](https://foss4g.nl)

Voting takes place via a standard online form. 
You can view all nominees [here](https://geoinformatienederland.nl/over-ons/gin-awards/geo-prestige-award-2026-de-genomineerden). 

Be sure to check out the other categories too:

* [3DGI](https://3dgi.nl/) is our favorite Startup, with many links to OSGeo.nl. 
* And: [Britta Ricker](https://www.uu.nl/medewerkers/BARicker) in the Professional category, see her book: [Mapping for a sustainable world](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3898826?v=pdf)!

So, don't let your vote go to waste. And, of course, we warmly invite you to share this message with others.

Thanks in advance! We’d love to take the stage at [GeoBuzz](https://geobuzz.nl/) on Nov 16, 2026!
